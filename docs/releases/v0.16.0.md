# WRAITER 0.16.0 — Wording guidance and revision tools

Find the words that carry the feeling you want. Selection alternatives now explain their emphasis, rate your existing wording, and let you describe the direction you want the AI to take. This Windows release also includes the manuscript revision tools developed since the previous public release, 0.13.0.

## Wording suggestions

- **Descriptions under alternatives** explain a choice's tone, meaning or emphasis. These short notes come from your selected model and stay separate from manuscript text.
- **Describe your vibe**, beside **More alternatives**, opens a text field for your intended feeling, viewpoint or style. **Find alternatives** regenerates choices for the same selection using that direction; subsequent retries retain it.
- **Current phrase rating** uses the same three-star scale as the suggestions. **Highest rated** or **Tied highest** indicates when the model considers your wording as strong as the rated alternatives. A model can recommend keeping your phrase without proposing a change. Missing ratings remain **Unrated**.
- **Settings → Writing AI → Selection suggestions** has separate targets of **1–8** for translations, corrections and rephrasings. Defaults are **3 / 1 / 3**. These are targets rather than guaranteed counts; corrections remain limited to spelling, punctuation and necessary grammar.
- Corrections use the same alternatives menu and their configured correction model. Translation and rephrasing still use the selected text and nearby context, and suggested wording stays a preview until accepted.

## Revision tools included since 0.13.0

- **Find and replace across chapters**, with case and whole-word filters, a preview of every replacement, and one undo step for a complete batch.
- **Private passage comments** that follow edits, can be resolved or reopened, and stay out of AI context and manuscript exports.
- **Copy, paste and clear formatting**, plus preset and custom highlight colours with readable backgrounds in every theme.
- **Native right-click actions** for formatting, comments, corrections, rephrasing, continuations and the writing assistant. The menu preserves the passage you selected.
- **Word-sized undo and redo**, steady undo scrolling, automatic checkpoints and a complete branching version tree. A separately chosen model can summarize version changes.
- **Windows Explorer opening and file associations**, including opening a manuscript in an already running WRAITER window and reusing existing tabs.
- **An updated README with eight fresh screenshots**, showing the writing workspace, selection tools, settings, assistant, proofreading and tutorial with fictional text and example responses.
