# WRAITER 0.20.1

Local Windows preview, 4 November 2026.

With **AI grammar hints while typing** enabled, hover over a purple underline to see the issue type, the proofreading model’s explanation (when supplied), and the suggested wording. A deletion is labelled explicitly. The preview reuses the existing finding and leaves the manuscript, selection and keyboard focus alone.

Move away or press **Esc** to dismiss the explanation. Click the underline to open the existing Apply/Ignore controls. Local grammar hints still work independently.
